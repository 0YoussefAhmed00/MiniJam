# 🌀 Motlazmet El Atbaa

Motlazmet El Atbaa is a physics-driven 2D platformer where the player must help Gayaar cross a mysterious city full of hazards and unpredictable effects.
Guide Gayaar through the city without succumbing to the mysterious Atbaa effect. Stay alert — the world may turn against you!

## 🧩 Key Features

- Psycho Mode — Periodic disruptions invert inputs, shake the camera, and change visuals

- Dynamic Obstacles — Dogs, buses, sewers, and more hazards that can interrupt your run

- Immersive Audio — Spatialized sounds, environmental cues, and reactive music

- Win & Lose Flows — Trigger success or failure sequences with unique animations
